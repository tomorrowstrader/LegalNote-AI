## Pre-Pilot Checklist

Before your first client meeting, complete these steps:

### ☐ 1. Set Up Your Firm Profile (5 minutes)
**Why:** Ensures all client documents carry your firm's branding

**How:**
1. Click your profile icon (top right) → **"Firm Settings"**
2. Fill in:
   - Firm name
   - Address
   - Phone and email
   - Upload firm logo (PNG/JPG, recommended 200x200px)
   - Upload letterhead image (optional, for PDF backgrounds)
3. Click **"Save Changes"**

**Check:** Preview a sample PDF to verify your branding looks correct.

---

### ☐ 2. Configure Your User Profile (2 minutes)
**Why:** Personalizes your experience and sets up calendar integration

**How:**
1. Click your profile icon → **"My Profile"**
2. Complete:
   - First name and last name
   - Professional title (e.g., "Senior Solicitor")
   - Practice areas (e.g., "Family Law, Wills & Probate")
3. Click **"Save Changes"**

---

### ☐ 3. Connect Your Calendar (Optional, 3 minutes)
**Why:** Sync case deadlines to Google Calendar or Outlook

**How:**
1. Go to **Settings** → **Calendar Integration** tab
2. Choose **"Connect Google Calendar"** or **"Connect Outlook"**
3. Sign in and authorize access
4. Select which calendar to sync to

**Note:** This is optional but highly recommended for deadline tracking.

---

### ☐ 4. Review the Interactive Tour (5 minutes)
**Why:** Quick overview of key features

**How:**
1. The tour launches automatically on first login
2. If you missed it: Click your profile icon → **"Restart Tour"**
3. Follow the interactive walkthrough

---

### ☐ 5. Test Audio Recording (2 minutes)
**Why:** Ensure your microphone works before a real client meeting

**How:**
1. Click **"Quick Record"** button (microphone icon in top navigation)
2. Grant browser permission to access microphone
3. Record 10 seconds of test audio
4. Click **"Stop Recording"**
5. Fill in test case details
6. Verify audio plays back correctly

**Troubleshooting:**
- **No microphone access:** Check browser permissions (usually a camera icon in address bar)
- **Poor audio quality:** Use a USB microphone or headset instead of built-in mic
- **Audio won't play:** Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)

## Your First Client Meeting (Step-by-Step)

### Before the Meeting

**☐ Prepare your client:**
*"I'd like to record our meeting today to ensure I capture all details accurately. This allows me to give you my full attention instead of taking notes. The recording is only used to create your attendance note and is automatically deleted after 7 days. Is that okay with you?"*

**☐ Set up your device:**
- Laptop or desktop (recording works best on computers, not phones)
- Chrome browser recommended
- Microphone tested and working
- LegalNote open in a tab

---

### During the Meeting

**Step 1: Start Recording (30 seconds)**
1. Click **"Quick Record"** (microphone icon) or **"New Note"** in navigation
2. Read the consent screen to your client or share your screen
3. Click **"I Confirm Client Has Consented"**
4. Click **"Start Recording"**
5. **Say out loud:** *"This is [your name] meeting with [client name] on [today's date]. [Client], you've consented to this recording, is that correct?"*
6. Client responds "Yes" (captured in audio)

**Step 2: Conduct Your Meeting Normally**
- Give the client your full attention
- No need to take detailed notes
- Speak clearly and naturally
- Glance at recording indicator occasionally to ensure it's still running

**Step 3: End Recording (10 seconds)**
1. When meeting concludes, click **"Stop Recording"**
2. Audio is saved automatically

**Step 4: Fill in Case Details (2 minutes)**
1. Client name
2. Case type (e.g., "Conveyancing", "Employment Dispute")
3. Matter reference (if you have one)
4. Brief description
5. Priority level (Normal/Urgent/Deadline Soon)
6. Deadline date (optional - if case has a deadline)
7. Click **"Save Case"**

**Upload Status:**
The system uploads the audio to secure cloud storage in the background. You'll see a progress indicator.

---

### After the Meeting (AI Processing)

**Step 5: Generate Transcript (2-5 minutes, automatic)**
1. Go to **Dashboard** or **Saved Cases**
2. Find your case and click on it
3. In the **Transcripts** tab, click **"Transcribe Audio"**
4. AI transcription begins (OpenAI Whisper processes the audio)
5. Wait 2-5 minutes depending on recording length
6. Transcript appears in the **Transcripts** tab

**What you see:**
- Full searchable transcript
- Timestamps for each section
- Speaker identification (if multiple speakers)
- Redaction tools (to hide sensitive info)

---

**Step 6: Generate Attendance Note (1-2 minutes, automatic)**
1. In the **Documents** tab, click **"Generate Attendance Note"**
2. AI generates a professional attendance note based on the transcript
3. Review the generated note
4. Edit as needed (click **"Edit"**)
5. When satisfied, click **"Mark as Reviewed"**

**What you see:**
A professional attendance note including:
- Date and attendees
- Background
- Matters discussed
- Actions required
- Next steps

---

**Step 7: Generate Legal Opinion (Optional, 1-2 minutes)**
1. In the **Documents** tab, click **"Generate Legal Opinion"**
2. AI generates a draft legal opinion based on meeting context
3. Review and edit thoroughly (this requires more review than attendance notes)
4. Add case law references, statutory citations, etc.
5. Click **"Mark as Reviewed"**

**Note:** Legal opinions require careful review. The AI provides a first draft - you provide the expertise.

---

**Step 8: Send to Client (30 seconds)**
1. In the **Documents** tab, find the document you want to share
2. Click **"Share"** → **"Create Share Link"**
3. Configure:
   - Recipient name (client's name)
   - Recipient email (client's email)
   - Expiration (recommend 7 days)
   - Password protection (optional but recommended)
   - SMS 2FA (optional - requires client's phone number)
4. Click **"Create Link"**
5. Click **"Send via Email"**

**What your client receives:**
- Professional email with your firm branding
- Secure link to access the document
- Password (if you set one)
- Expiration notice

## Best Practices for Success

### Recording Quality
✓ **Use an external microphone** (USB mic or headset) for best results
✓ **Minimize background noise** (close doors, turn off fans)
✓ **Speak clearly** and ask clients to do the same
✓ **Position microphone** equidistant between you and client
✗ Don't use built-in laptop mic if meeting across large desk
✗ Don't record in noisy environments (cafes, open offices)

### Consent Management
✓ **Always obtain verbal consent** at the start of recording
✓ **Explain the benefits** to clients (accuracy, your full attention)
✓ **Mention automatic deletion** (7 days) for privacy reassurance
✗ Don't skip the consent screen
✗ Don't assume implied consent

### Document Review
✓ **Always review AI-generated documents** before sending to clients
✓ **Check dates, names, and facts** for accuracy
✓ **Add case law and citations** to legal opinions
✓ **Use firm-specific language** and templates where needed
✗ Don't send unreviewed AI documents to clients
✗ Don't rely 100% on AI - it's a first draft

### Security Practices
✓ **Log out when leaving your desk** (or wait for 4-hour auto-logout)
✓ **Use strong passwords** (avoid password reuse)
✓ **Set expiration dates** on share links (7 days recommended)
✓ **Enable SMS 2FA** for highly sensitive documents
✗ Don't share passwords with colleagues
✗ Don't leave sensitive cases open on screen overnight

## Common Questions

### "How accurate is the transcription?"
OpenAI Whisper achieves 95-99% accuracy depending on audio quality. Clear audio with minimal background noise yields the best results.

### "Can I edit the transcript?"
Not directly, but you can redact sensitive information (names, addresses) before sharing. The transcript is primarily for reference.

### "What if the AI gets something wrong in the attendance note?"
Always review and edit before sending. The AI provides a first draft based on the transcript - you add the legal expertise.

### "How long are recordings kept?"
7 days. After that, they're automatically deleted for GDPR compliance. Transcripts and documents remain indefinitely.

### "Can I access LegalNote on my phone?"
Yes, but recording works best on desktop/laptop. You can view cases and documents on mobile.

### "What happens if I lose internet during recording?"
The recording continues locally in your browser. It will upload once connection is restored.

### "Can multiple solicitors share the same case?"
Not in the current pilot version - each case belongs to the solicitor who created it. Team sharing is on the roadmap.

### "What if my client doesn't consent to recording?"
Skip the recording and use LegalNote for document storage and management only. You can manually upload documents.
