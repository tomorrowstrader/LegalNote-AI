LegalNote turns client meetings and internal discussions into structured, contemporaneous attendance records that already reflect how regulators expect legal work to be evidenced. ## 3. “How it works” (without sounding like software)

### Section header  
Attendance records, formed at source

### Body copy (improved)

LegalNote supports practitioners by capturing client meetings and internal discussions through consent‑first workflows designed for UK‑regulated legal environments.  Conversations are securely recorded and reviewed, then formed into a structured attendance note that reflects instructions, advice, decisions, and follow‑up actions in a way that aligns with SRA expectations for contemporaneous records.[4][1][3]

The practitioner remains in control at every stage: LegalNote proposes the structure and content of the attendance note, but professional judgement determines what is kept, amended, or removed before the record is finalised.[2][4]

**What changed vs. your previous version**

- Explicit “UK‑regulated legal environments” so it feels **for solicitors**, not generic SaaS.[4]
- Names SRA expectations around contemporaneous, detailed records without becoming legalese.[1][3][4]
- States professional control plainly, which reduces “AI making decisions” anxiety.

## 4. The “magic” section (actions, diaries, continuity)

### Section header  
Decisions don’t get lost. Actions don’t drift.

### Body copy (improved)

LegalNote identifies decisions, next steps, and responsibilities as they arise in conversation, so they are not buried in a long transcript or forgotten notebook.  Actions can be surfaced, diarised, and synced into existing workflows, while remaining linked back to the attendance record and matter history, so the evolution of advice and instructions is always visible.[3][2]

Instead of treating attendance notes as static files, LegalNote helps create a living record of the matter: what was known, what was agreed, and why specific actions were taken at each stage.[2][4]

**Why this sells the magic better**

- Emphasises “not buried in a transcript / notebook” – that is what dictation and generic note‑takers leave you with.[6][7]
- Introduces “living record of the matter” which fits your “signature of the work” metaphor but stays concrete.[4][2]
- Makes the diary integration part of the compliance story (evidence of follow‑up), not just convenience.

## 5. Compliance & consent (without AI hype)

### Section header  
Built to evidence professional judgement

### Body copy (improved)

LegalNote is designed around the reality that detailed attendance notes are a core strand of evidencing competent service and defensible decision‑making.  Records are timestamped, contemporaneous, and retained in line with firm policy, creating a clear audit trail of instructions, advice, and actions over time.[5][8][1][3][4]

Consent‑first workflows make it straightforward to explain, obtain, and record client consent to recording, so practitioners can use modern tools without cutting against expectations of confidentiality, transparency, or client understanding.[1][4]

**How this addresses your worries**

- Makes “compliance‑born record” explicit.[3][4]
- Talks about consent as a **workflow** feature, not a legal disclaimer line item.[1][4]
- Avoids “AI” language entirely – the emphasis is on evidencing judgement, not automation.

## 6. Differentiation from dictation & generic note‑takers

Use this angle wherever you need to “compare without naming” (website, decks, docs). It translates directly into comparison tables, FAQ answers, and sales talk‑tracks.

### Framing sentence

LegalNote is not another note‑taking app or dictation tool; it is a compliance‑first attendance record system built for regulated legal practice.

### Suggested comparison table

Title: How LegalNote differs from generic note tools

| Dimension                  | Typical dictation / note apps                                                                 | LegalNote                                                                                                                 |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

| Legal domain awareness    | Little or no awareness of legal duties, SRA guidance, or evidential standards. [6][9] | Built around the role of attendance notes in evidencing competent service and defensible decision‑making. [1][4] |

- “Forms a **reviewable** attendance note” instead of “drafts an attendance note”.[2]

- “Identifies and surfaces actions and decisions” instead of “auto‑extracts tasks”.[3]
