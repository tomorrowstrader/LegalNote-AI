Visual & Style System
General


Overall aesthetic: premium SaaS for legal teams. Think “Stripe Dashboard + Notion + Apple”.


Rounded corners: use a consistent radius (e.g. rounded-xl / rounded-2xl) for cards and buttons.


Spacing: generous and consistent (p-4, p-6, gap-4 etc.).


Use a clear hierarchy of font sizes and weights:


Page title: bold, large (text-2xl or text-3xl)


Section titles: text-lg / text-xl


Card labels and metadata: text-xs–text-sm




All text should meet WCAG AA contrast against its background.


Colours
Define a small design token set (using Tailwind config extension or CSS variables):


Primary Brand Gold: warm but not washed out (e.g. #D8AC4A or similar)


Primary Navy / Slate: deep, not grey (e.g. #0B1020 / #111827)


Light Background: off-white / very light neutral, not yellow (#F5F5F7 etc.)


Card Background (Light): #FFFFFF with subtle tinted gradient or very soft warm tint.


Card Background (Dark): slightly lighter navy/indigo with subtle inner glow.


Accent Badges:


Action Required: red (e.g. #E74C3C)


Deadline Approaching: amber (#F39C12)


Reviewed: green (#27AE60)




Avoid overly pale yellows. The new light theme should feel clean, neutral and professional with gold as a highlight, not as the base background.
Shadows & Depth
The previous design felt washed out. Fix that:


Use clear but soft shadows on cards:


e.g. Tailwind: shadow-[0_14px_30px_rgba(15,23,42,0.12)] for main cards.




Distinguish layers:


Nav bar above background


KPI stat cards slightly higher


Priority cards and recent cards clearly separated from background




Avoid heavy blur; go for crisp, directional shadows.
