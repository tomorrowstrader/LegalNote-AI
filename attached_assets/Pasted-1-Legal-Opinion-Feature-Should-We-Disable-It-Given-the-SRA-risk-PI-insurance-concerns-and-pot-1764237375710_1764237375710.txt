3. Email Panel vs Document Panel – Clarification for MVP

Your suggestion about an email timeline panel is excellent, and prompted me to have an idea, but I’m unsure whether it belongs in MVP/MCP.

Many meetings require reviewing documents, evidence, PDFs, attachments, etc. (usually beforehand).

I’ve had experience of Solicitors receiving these via Google Drive links, zipped folders, or direct uploads. I don’t know whether there are more official PMS-like workflows widely utilised for the process by bigger firm, but I assume a legal 500 firm isn’t just receiving case related docuemtn via gmail from clients (but I could be wrong).

Questions:

a. Can we create a “Document Panel” similar to the email panel?
One that lets the solicitor:
	•	view relevant documents,
	•	ask questions about them,
	•	generate prep notes,
	•	extract key topics before the meeting?

b. Can LegalNote assist the solicitor in becoming “meeting-ready” by analysing these documents prior to the meeting?

c. How would we implement this while respecting security/SRA requirements?
Does this require hosting documents inside LegalNote, or can we safely pull them from external storage (Drive, SharePoint, PMS document vault) without storing copies?

d. Is document handling viable for MVP, or does it risk scope creep?
