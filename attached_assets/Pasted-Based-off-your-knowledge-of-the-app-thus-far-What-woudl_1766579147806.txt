For UK boutiques and solos, pricing needs to feel modest versus a single fee‑earner’s billable day, and clearly tied to risk reduction rather than “nice‑to‑have AI”. A sensible starting point is a hybrid per‑user SaaS + usage model, with softer entry for solos.

Core structure
Per‑user, per‑month licence (tiered by firm size)

Solo / 1–2 users: £40–£60 per user / month.

Small boutique (3–20 fee‑earners using the tool): £35–£50 per user / month.

Include: recording, transcription pipeline, note generation, action extraction, audit trails, calendar/PMS integrations, and standard support.

Fair‑use or metered usage component

Bundle a generous monthly recording allowance per user (e.g. 20–30 recorded meeting hours).

Above that, overage at a small per‑hour rate (e.g. £2–£4 / recorded hour) so heavy users cover extra transcription costs without scaring light users.

Solo‑friendly and boutique‑friendly elements
Solo “chambers‑style” plan

Flat fee (e.g. £60–£80 / month) for 1 user with enough hours baked in that they never think about overage in normal use.

Month‑to‑month or 3‑month minimum, to lower commitment anxiety.

Boutique risk & compliance pack

For 5+ users, offer: one‑off onboarding fee (e.g. £500–£1,500) that includes help with policies, DPIA templates, and insurer‑facing documentation, which you can explicitly position as part of their risk infrastructure rather than set‑up “IT fees”.

Why this structure fits UK boutiques / solos
Keeps headline cost in the low hundreds per month for a small firm, which is comparable to other core SaaS tools and easy to justify against even a single avoided complaint or claim.

Aligns cost with actual recording intensity, which matters because usage will vary widely by practice area and seniority.

Allows you to frame pricing in risk language: “for ~£X per fee‑earner per month you get standardised, auditable attendance notes and action capture for all recorded meetings”, not “pay £££ for AI features”.

If you like, the next step can be to draft a one‑page pricing sheet you could show to 2–3 friendly boutiques and ask, “Is this in the right ballpark for you?” and adjust from their feedback.
