## 1. Practical process to produce the video

**A. Plan the narrative (before recording)**  
- Define a single story: “Simulated client call → auto transcription → AI attendance note and actions → secure sharing link.”  
- Script 90–120 seconds of spoken narration plus 30–60 seconds of slower UI moments (hovering, highlighting data protection, etc.).  
- Decide 2–3 practice areas you want to visually hint at (e.g. housing/family/commercial) but record one clear path for v1.

**B. Capture the golden-path screen recording**  
- Use a high-quality recorder that supports 1080p–4K, webcam bubble, and system audio, such as:  
  - Loom desktop app (1080p/4K, very fast to iterate).[8][9][10][11][7]
  - ScreenFlow or Camtasia if you want more precise zoom/pan and callouts.[12][4][1]
  - VibrantSnap or similar “cinematic” tools if you want AI-assisted pacing and visual polish.[2]
- Record on a clean staging tenant with:  
  - Obvious fake client details.  
  - Only the features you want to show (hide admin clutter).  
- Structure the recording in 5 segments (easy to edit):  
  1) Opening dashboard.  
  2) Starting/ingesting a call recording.  
  3) Transcription view (with diarisation and consent marker visible).  
  4) AI-generated attendance note & summary.  
  5) Action items + secure sharing preview.

**C. Polish the edit**  
- Use the same tool’s editor (Loom trim, ScreenFlow/Camtasia timeline, or VibrantSnap AI pacing).[4][11][1][2]
- Add:  
  - Subtle zoom‑ins on key compliance touches (consent banner, SRA/GDPR‑friendly wording).  
  - On‑screen labels like “Auto‑generated from your call” or “Client‑safe share link”.  
  - 1–2 second pauses on important screens so it doesn’t feel rushed.

## 3. How to structure the interactive tour

**A. Break the 2–3 minute demo into “chapters”**  
- Example chapters:  
  1) “Meet LegalNote AI” – what problem it solves.  
  2) “From call to transcript” – recording and diarisation.  
  3) “AI attendance note” – style, structure, and compliance hints.  
  4) “Action items & secure sharing” – how it turns into workflows.  
- Each chapter is a short clip or screen sequence with:  
  - A 1–2 sentence caption.  
  - Optional audio voiceover (same voice as main video) or AI voiceover (some tour tools now support this).[15][3][5]

**B. Layer interactions without exposing real product data**  
- Use hotspots:  
  - “Click to see how consent is captured” → jumps to the consent marker on the transcript.  
  - “Click to see the generated tasks” → reveals the action panel.  
- Add inline explanations:  
  - Callout bubbles for “Speaker labels from diarisation” or “Configurable templates for COLP/COFA.”  
- Gate advanced chapters behind a mini form (“See full note” → email capture), if desired.

## 4. Ways to add “wow factor” without complexity

**A. Tight narrative tailored to solicitors**  
- Start with a bold claim: “In under 3 minutes, see how LegalNote turns a client call into a file‑ready attendance note and action list.”  
- Emphasise risk reduction: show how mis‑notes are avoided, how speaker labels and time stamps support auditability, and how templates align with SRA/GDPR expectations.

**B. Visual emphasis on compliance**  
- Zoom in on:  
  - Explicit consent capture UI.  
  - Data retention settings or a “Demo data only” tag.  
  - Clear “No client data required for this demo” messaging.  
- Use on-screen captions like “Designed for SRA‑regulated firms” or “Built with consent and audit trails from day one.”

**C. Before/after contrast in 10 seconds**  
- Show a messy, fictional text doc labeled “Manual note taking” vs. your structured LegalNote attendance note and task list.  
- Use a quick split-screen moment: left = static Word doc; right = your structured note and actions populating instantly.

**D. Subtle motion and branding touches**  
- Use cursor highlights, gentle zooms, and branded colour overlays from tools like ScreenFlow, VibrantSnap, or your interactive tour platform.[2][3][4][5]
- Keep motion purposeful: each movement should draw attention to a specific benefit (not just eye candy).

**E. Optional: embedded micro‑interactions alongside the video**  
On the page, alongside the video/tour, add:  
- A “Choose your practice area” toggle that swaps in slightly different pre-recorded tours (e.g., housing vs family) while reusing 80% of the same footage.  
- A one-click “Email me this example note” CTA that sends them a PDF/HTML export of the demo note, reinforcing that LegalNote can produce externally shareable documents.

   - Define chapters.  
   - Add hotspots, captions, and branching where appropriate.[6][16][3][4][5]
