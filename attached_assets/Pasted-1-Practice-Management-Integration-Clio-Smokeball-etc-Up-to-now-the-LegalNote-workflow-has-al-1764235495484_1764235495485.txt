1. Practice Management Integration (Clio, Smokeball, etc.)

Up to now, the LegalNote workflow has always assumed:
	•	The solicitor opens LegalNote right before the meeting,
	•	They either use Quick Record or
	•	Create a new case, which immediately sets them up to start recording the meeting.

So when you suggested:

“…before the meeting the solicitor selects the client/matter in LegalNote…”

I need clarity on how that would technically work because all client data, matter details, documents and past notes live inside the practice management system, not LegalNote.

My core questions for clarity:

a. Where does LegalNote fetch the context from?
Are we imagining that LegalNote imports:
	•	client details
	•	matter details
	•	previous attendance notes
	•	documents
	•	key dates

directly from Clio/Smokeball, and displays them when a solicitor clicks “Start New Meeting”?

b. What happens when a solicitor creates a case in LegalNote before they’ve had a meeting?
Since a matter can exist in Clio without any meeting scheduled yet, how do we map:
	•	Clio matter → LegalNote context
	•	without prematurely creating “meeting sessions” in either system?

c. What’s the exact moment where context-loading should happen?
Options I’m considering:
	1.	When a solicitor creates a new Case/Meeting inside LegalNote
	2.	When they select an existing matter already synced from the PMS
	3.	Or only after they actually press “Start Recording”
I need clarity on which moment makes the workflow simplest and avoids doubling work for them.

Right now this integration seems powerful, but requires deeper thinking so it truly solves a real solicitor workflow bottleneck, not adds steps.

2. Email Integration (Gmail / Outlook)

The concept is extremely strong — solicitors constantly search through massive email threads to reconstruct the “story” of a matter.

So the idea that LegalNote can show the full email timeline inside the meeting workspace is very appealing.

My main question:

How do we determine which emails belong to the matter?

Options could include:
	1.	All emails to/from the client
But this risks including irrelevant messages.
	2.	Emails with the matter reference in the subject line
Many firms use unique matter IDs (e.g. “REF: 44329-1”).
	3.	Emails manually “linked” from their PMS
Clio, for example, lets users associate specific emails with a matter.
	4.	Rule-based automatic linking
e.g.

	•	Sender = client
	•	Subject contains keywords
	•	Thread previously linked to matter
	•	Or they tag the email in Outlook/Gmail (“Assign to LegalNote Matter”)

Before we design it, I need clarity on whether:
	•	LegalNote automatically pulls everything,
	•	Or whether
	•	The solicitor needs to confirm which emails belong.

This decision affects how powerful — and how safe — email integration will be
