## Post 1: The Shak Story (Early Visibility)

**Angle:** Commercial loss from late visibility — not regulation.

**Draft:**

A partner told me about a deal that ran six months.

Three people touched it. At the final review, he found a required client conversation had never happened.

He called the client to explain — at the most sensitive point in the matter.

The client said they hadn't understood. They hadn't been told.

They pulled out.

The problem wasn't negligence. Nobody could see what happened in the meetings until the end.

If that gap had been visible in week two, the conversation would have happened while trust was still intact.

This is the pattern I hear from growing firms — not "will the SRA audit us?" but "how do I know what's happening in meetings I'm not in?"

You have a system for your pipeline. A system for your matters.

LegalNote is Meeting-to-Matter — your system for client meetings. Every conference captured, documented, on the file.
