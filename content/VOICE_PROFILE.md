## WHAT LEGALNOTE IS

**One-Liner:** A compliance-first legal documentation platform for UK solicitors.

**Positioning:** LegalNote is NOT "another AI tool for lawyers." It is a defensive weapon against PI claims and regulatory exposure.

**Core Value Proposition:**
- Treats every client meeting like evidence (because in litigation, it is)
- Contemporaneous documentation (created in real-time, not from memory)
- Consent-first workflow (verification baked into the audio)
- Audit trails with cryptographic signatures (tamper-evident)

**EU Data Residency:**
- Audio transcription: Dublin, Ireland (AssemblyAI EU)
- Meeting recording: Frankfurt, Germany (Recall.ai EU)
- Database/Hosting: EU (Neon, Replit)
- Document generation: US with Standard Contractual Clauses (OpenAI)

**Target Market:**
- Solo practitioners and boutique law firms (1-10 solicitors)
- UK-based, SRA-regulated
- Firms who care about compliance and defensibility

**What We're NOT:**
- We don't provide legal advice
- We don't do legal analysis
- We're a documentation tool, not a practice management system

## THE SLAY FRAMEWORK (Lara Acosta)

Use this structure for every post:

**S - Story:** Start with a story to capture attention. Personal experience, client scenario, industry observation.

**L - Lesson:** Lead with the lesson for retention. What's the insight? The pattern? The truth nobody talks about?

**A - Actionable Advice:** Give tactical, actionable steps. Specifics, not generalities. Numbers and lists work well.

**Y - You:** End by pointing it back to THEM. A question, a challenge, a call to action. Make them feel seen.

## ADDITIONAL FRAMEWORKS

### The 8-Word Hook Rule
- First line: 8 words maximum (mobile cut-off)
- Second line (re-hook): Adds specificity or numbers
- "How I" > "How to" (personal credibility beats generic advice)

### Broad → Narrow → Niche
Within each post:
1. **Broad hook:** Universal appeal, reaches many
2. **Narrow problem:** Specific to target audience
3. **Niche solution:** Your unique methodology

*Example: "I built my first app 4 months ago" (broad) → "Today, 5 London law firms use it daily" (narrow) → "A compliance-first documentation system" (niche)*

### PAS Framework (Problem-Agitate-Solution)
Quick format for direct posts:
- **Problem:** State it plainly
- **Agitate:** Make it worse (the consequences)
- **Solution:** Provide the answer with steps
