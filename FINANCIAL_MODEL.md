# LegalNote AI - Financial Model & Investor Summary

## Executive Summary

LegalNote AI is a compliance-first legal documentation platform targeting UK solicitors and boutique law firms. The platform transforms meeting recordings into evidence-quality attendance notes, AI summaries, and searchable transcripts while maintaining full GDPR compliance and SRA alignment.

**Key Investment Highlights:**
- **70%+ gross margins** on core subscriptions
- **Negative churn potential** through firm-wide expansion
- **£1.7M+ ARR achievable** with 2,500 users (~1.4% of UK solicitor market)
- **Defensible moat** through compliance positioning and audit trail lock-in

## Competitive Advantages

### 1. Compliance-First Positioning
- Not "AI for lawyers" but "risk infrastructure"
- COLP/risk partner audience vs. IT buyer
- PI insurance narrative differentiator

### 2. Audit Trail Lock-In
- Cryptographic signatures on all events
- Tamper-evident logging
- SRA inquiry readiness = switching cost

### 3. UK Legal Specificity
- Word boost for UK legal terminology
- GDPR-first architecture
- SRA alignment documentation

### 4. Solicitor-Controlled AI
- "AI-assisted, not AI-decided" principle
- All outputs require solicitor approval
- No cross-client training
