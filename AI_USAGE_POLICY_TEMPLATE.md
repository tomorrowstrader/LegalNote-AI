## [FIRM NAME] AI Usage Policy: LegalNote AI Platform

### 1. Scope

This policy governs the use of LegalNote AI for meeting recording, transcription, attendance note generation, and action item extraction.

**Applies to:**
- All fee-earners (partners, associates, trainees)
- Support staff with platform access
- Any person using the platform on behalf of the firm

**Effective date:** [DATE]

### 2. Permitted Uses

LegalNote AI may be used for:

| Use | Permitted | Conditions |
|-----|-----------|------------|
| Recording client meetings | Yes | With client consent |
| Recording internal meetings | Yes | With participant consent |
| Generating attendance notes | Yes | Subject to solicitor review |
| Extracting action items | Yes | Subject to solicitor review |
| Sharing documents with clients | Yes | Via approved share link with 2FA |

### 3. Prohibited Uses

LegalNote AI must NOT be used for:

| Prohibited Use | Reason |
|----------------|--------|
| Recording without consent | GDPR and ethical requirement |
| Substituting professional judgment | AI outputs are documentation aids only |
| Sharing unreviewed AI outputs | All outputs require solicitor approval |
| Recording prohibited matter types | See Section 7 |
| Circumventing approval workflows | Governance requirement |

### 4. Solicitor Accountability

**Fundamental principle:** The solicitor remains fully accountable for all outputs.

- AI-generated attendance notes are DRAFTS until approved
- AI-extracted action items are CANDIDATES until confirmed
- The solicitor must review, edit if necessary, and approve all outputs
- Approved outputs become the solicitor's professional work product
- The AI does not provide legal advice, analysis, or recommendations

**Quote for training purposes:**
> "LegalNote AI documents what you said and did. It does not tell you what to say or do. You remain the lawyer; the platform is your documentation tool."

### 5. Consent Requirements

Before recording any meeting:

1. **Explain to all participants** that the meeting will be recorded
2. **Explain the purpose** - transcription and attendance note generation
3. **Explain AI involvement** - the recording will be processed by AI services
4. **Obtain consent** - use the platform's consent flow
5. **Document consent** - the platform logs consent with exact wording

**Consent script (suggested wording):**
> "I'm going to record this meeting using our documentation platform, LegalNote AI. The recording will be transcribed and used to generate an attendance note. The audio is processed by AI services and deleted after 7 days. The transcript and notes are retained on your matter file. Do you consent to this recording?"

If any participant declines, do not record. Use manual note-taking.

### 6. Review and Approval Obligations

| Output | Review Required | Approver | Before |
|--------|-----------------|----------|--------|
| Transcript | Recommended | Fee-earner | Note generation |
| Attendance note | **Mandatory** | Supervising solicitor | Filing/export |
| Action items | **Mandatory** | Supervising solicitor | Diary entry |

**Supervision of junior fee-earners:**
- Trainees and junior associates must have outputs reviewed by a supervisor
- The supervisor's approval is logged in the audit trail
- Supervision applies to all AI-generated outputs

### 8. Data Handling Obligations

All users must understand:

| Obligation | Requirement |
|------------|-------------|
| Audio retention | 7 days then automatically deleted |
| Transcript retention | Matter lifetime (unless deleted earlier) |
| No external sharing | Do not copy transcripts to external AI tools |
| No workarounds | Do not use personal devices to record |
| Litigation holds | Apply when proceedings anticipated |

### 9. Confidentiality and Privilege

- All data processed by LegalNote AI remains confidential
- Privilege attaches to outputs as it would to manual attendance notes
- Do not process data that should not leave the firm's systems
- Sub-processors (AssemblyAI, OpenAI) are contractually bound to confidentiality
- No client data is used to train AI models

### 10. Incident Reporting

Report immediately to [COLP/IT SECURITY] if:

| Incident Type | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| Unintended recording | Stop recording, report, assess deletion |
| Consent dispute | Report, preserve evidence, seek guidance |
| Inaccurate output relied upon | Report, assess impact, document |
| Suspected data breach | Report immediately, do not investigate alone |
| Platform access by unauthorised person | Report, suspend access if possible |

## Appendix A: Quick Reference Card

**Before Recording:**
1. Explain recording to all participants
2. Explain AI processing
3. Obtain consent via platform flow
4. If anyone declines, use manual notes

**After Recording:**
1. Review transcript for accuracy
2. Review attendance note - edit if needed
3. Approve attendance note before filing
4. Review action items - confirm or delete
5. Approve action items before diary entry

**Never:**
- Record without consent
- File unreviewed AI outputs
- Share transcripts externally
- Use personal recording devices
- Assume AI outputs are correct without review
