# LegalNote — 60-Second Growth Demo Script

> **Use on:** Discovery calls, live demos, webinar openers, partner champion meetings.
> **Audience:** Managing partners at UK firms turning £750k–£1.5m, 4–8 fee earners.
> **Rule:** Demo a scenario. Never demo features. Stop at 60 seconds unless they ask for more.

---

## The one question (before you open the product)

> "When you're not in the client meeting — how do you know the file reflects what was actually said?"

Pause. Let them answer. Then:

> "That's what LegalNote solves. Let me show you one matter."

---

## 60-second demo flow

### Beat 1 — Open the matter (10 sec)

**Say:**
> "This is a six-month employment matter — the kind that runs through two or three fee earners before you ever see the file."

**Show:** Case timeline with multiple meeting sessions visible.

---

### Beat 2 — Week-two meeting (15 sec)

**Say:**
> "Week two. Initial client conference. The fee earner records — stays present with the client. LegalNote produces the attendance note on the matter within minutes. Same format, every time."

**Show:** One session → attendance note → structured sections.

**Do not:** Name features. Do not tour the UI.

---

### Beat 3 — Partner review (20 sec)

**Say:**
> "Now you're the managing partner. You weren't in that meeting — but you can see the timeline. Every conference on the matter, in order. You inspect what you expect, without chasing anyone for a note typed from memory at six o'clock."

**Show:** Timeline with 2–3 sessions. Click into one note briefly.

**Optional (if they lean in):**
> "If a required conversation hadn't happened, you'd see the gap here — in week two. Not at completion. Not when the client is already spooked."

---

### Beat 4 — Close (15 sec)

**Say:**
> "That's Meeting-to-Matter. You have a system for your pipeline, a system for your matters — LegalNote is the system for your client meetings. Every conference captured, on the file, same standard whether it's you or your newest hire."

**Stop.** Wait for their question.

---

## If they ask "what about compliance / SRA?"

> "The attendance note is structured to SRA standards — consent captured, audit trail on every version. But that's the foundation. What partners actually feel is: I can grow the team without losing sight of what happened in the room."

---

## If they share a Shak-style story

**Reflect back:**
> "That's exactly the pattern — visibility arriving too late. If that conversation had been flagged in week two, you'd have handled it while the client still trusted the process."

**Do not** say "Shak told me" unless they've given permission to reference Penn Group.

---

## Discovery call opener (first 2 minutes)

> "Thanks for making time, [Name]. Before I show you anything — tell me about the firm. Are you growing the team at the moment?"

**Listen for:**
- Hiring / training new starters
- "I can't be in every meeting"
- Attendance notes eating time
- Referrals matter
- Past matter that went wrong at the end

**Transition:**
> "When you hire your next fee earner — how will you know their client meetings are documented to your standard?"

---

## Category language (use consistently)

| Say | Don't say |
|-----|-----------|
| Meeting-to-Matter™ | Meeting Management System |
| Your system for client meetings | AI tool for lawyers |
| Attendance notes on the matter | Automated legal analysis |
| Inspect what you expect | Compliance platform |
| Catch gaps early | SRA-ready (unless they bring up regulation) |

---

## CTA (end of call)

> "We run a 30-day governed evaluation — your team uses LegalNote on live matters, no annual commitment. Would it help to walk through what that looks like for a firm your size?"

---

## Demo anti-patterns

- Don't show more than one matter scenario
- Don't lead with AI, GDPR, or EU hosting unless they ask
- Don't demo AML/compliance thread by name — show the timeline and notes
- Don't run past 60 seconds without a pause for questions
- Don't pitch features they didn't ask about
