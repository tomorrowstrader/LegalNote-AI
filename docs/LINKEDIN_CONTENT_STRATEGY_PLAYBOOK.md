# LegalNote AI - LinkedIn Content Strategy Playbook

> **Purpose**: Comprehensive guide to LinkedIn posting frequency, content formats, and conversion tactics for building authority and generating leads.

---

## Why LinkedIn (And Why Not Other Platforms)

### LinkedIn Is Enough Because:

1. **Your target audience lives there**
   - Solicitors, practice managers, COLPs, managing partners
   - Legal ops professionals
   - Practice management consultants
   - They scroll LinkedIn during work hours, not TikTok

2. **B2B buying happens on LinkedIn**
   - Decision-makers research solutions on LinkedIn
   - Professional credibility matters more than entertainment value
   - Content can be repurposed for sales conversations

3. **Algorithm rewards consistency**
   - 12 weeks of consistent posting builds momentum
   - Small audience with high engagement beats large audience with low engagement

### Why NOT TikTok/YouTube (For Now):

1. **Different content format** - Entertainment vs professional
2. **Different audience** - Younger, consumer-focused
3. **Unsustainable** - Daily posting required for algorithm
4. **ROI timeline** - Much longer for B2B

### When YouTube Makes Sense (Month 6+):

- Product demo videos (embed on website)
- Workshop recordings (repurpose as lead magnets)
- "Meeting to Matter Framework" explainer (one evergreen video)

These are **sales assets**, not a content strategy.

## Posting Frequency

### Recommended: 3 Posts Per Week

- **Monday**: Start-of-week energy, people checking LinkedIn
- **Wednesday**: Mid-week engagement peak
- **Friday**: Lighter content, reflection pieces

### Why This Frequency:

- 1 post/week: Too slow, no algorithm momentum
- Daily: Unsustainable, quality suffers
- 3x/week: Consistent enough to grow, manageable to maintain

### With 30 Connections:

Your reach is currently limited, but that's fine. The algorithm expands reach based on **engagement rate**, not follower count.

**Priority**: Grow connections to 200-500 in your target audience. Connect with 10-15 people per week.

## Content Formats Ranked by Engagement

### Tier 1: Highest Engagement

**1. Text-Only Posts (150-300 words)**
- Strong hook in first line
- Line breaks for readability
- No external links (algorithm penalises)
- Clear, actionable insight

**2. Carousels (PDF Slides)**
- 5-10 slides maximum
- One idea per slide
- Minimal text per slide (15-20 words max)
- Strong title slide
- CTA on final slide

### Tier 2: Good Engagement

**3. Text + Single Image**
- Image should add context, not decoration
- Diagrams and frameworks work well
- Avoid stock photos (they look like ads)

**4. Polls**
- Good for engagement, use sparingly (1 per month)
- Ask genuinely interesting questions
- Follow up with insights from results

### Tier 3: Lower Organic Reach

**5. Video**
- Native video only (uploaded directly, not YouTube links)
- Under 2 minutes for casual content
- Save for demos and announcements

**6. External Links**
- Algorithm penalises posts with links
- If you must link, put link in comments
- Or use "Link in comments" approach

## Branding on Content

### For Carousels/PDFs:

- Simple branded header (LegalNote logo small, consistent colour)
- Your name on first and last slide
- Don't over-design - clean and readable beats fancy
- Consistent colour scheme throughout

### For Images:

- **NO stock photos** - They look like ads, people scroll past
- Simple diagrams or frameworks work well
- Screenshots of real product (when ready to show)
- Consistent visual style

### Talking Head Video (Later):

- Not necessary at this stage
- Phone camera is fine - authenticity beats production value
- Good lighting matters more than expensive equipment

## Content Categories That Convert

### 1. Problem-Aware Posts

Describe a pain point your audience feels. No solution yet, just recognition.

**Example**:
"Every solicitor has had that moment when a client says 'but you told me...' and your file note is three bullet points from two weeks ago.

The meeting is vivid in your memory. The problem is, memories aren't evidence.

What's your system for capturing meetings in real-time?"

### 2. Framework Posts

Teach something actionable. Provide immediate value.

**Example**:
"The 5-step documentation process that saved me from a client complaint:

1. PREPARE - Know your matter ref, have consent script ready
2. CAPTURE - Record or note in real-time, not from memory
3. PROCESS - Transcribe within 24 hours
4. DOCUMENT - Standard template, every time
5. PROTECT - Audit trail, version control, secure storage

Which step does your firm struggle with most?"

### 3. Contrarian Takes

Challenge common practice. Respectfully disagree with conventional wisdom.

**Example**:
"Unpopular opinion: Most law firms treat attendance notes as admin.

They're not admin. They're your first line of defence.

When a client disputes your advice three years from now, your attendance note isn't paperwork - it's evidence.

Treat it accordingly."

### 4. Story Posts

Share a specific experience (anonymised). Personal stories drive engagement.

**Example**:
"At a City firm, I watched a senior associate spend three hours reconstructing a meeting from memory.

The file note was two sentences. The meeting was three weeks ago. The client was disputing the advice.

Three hours of billable time. Zero certainty about what was actually said.

That's when I realised: this isn't a discipline problem. It's a tools problem.

What would you have done differently?"

### 5. Data/Research Posts

Share statistics or research findings. Position yourself as informed.

**Example**:
"Case law shows what happens when attendance notes fail:

Prime London v Withers: 'Contrary to reasonable professional practice'
Wellesley v Withers: 'Misunderstood, noted down wrong, or misremembered'
Alliance and Leicester: Solicitor could only 'speculate' about what was said

These aren't small firm cases. Withers is a major international firm.

The lesson: even the best get documentation wrong.

The question: what's your failsafe?"

## Content That Gets Vanity Metrics But Doesn't Convert

Avoid these (they get likes but no leads):

- Motivational content ("Believe in yourself!")
- Generic industry commentary ("Legal tech is changing!")
- Celebrating milestones nobody cares about ("We hit 100 users!")
- Reposting news without adding perspective
- Engagement bait ("Like if you agree!")

## The CTA Strategy

### Don't Hard-Sell in Every Post

**4 out of 5 posts**: No CTA, just value
**1 out of 5 posts**: Soft CTA

### Soft CTA Examples:

- "DM me if you want the checklist"
- "Comment 'framework' and I'll send the PDF"
- "Drop a question below and I'll answer it"
- "Tag a colleague who needs this"

### Hard CTA (Use Sparingly):

- "Link to [resource] in comments"
- "Book a call: [calendar link]"
- "Try LegalNote free: [link]"

Save hard CTAs for announcements, launches, and direct response campaigns.

## Engagement Strategy

### Daily Routine (15-20 minutes):

1. **Check notifications** - Respond to all comments on your posts
2. **Engage with 5-10 posts** from target audience - Meaningful comments, not just "Great post!"
3. **Send 3-5 connection requests** to people in your target market
4. **Check DMs** - Respond promptly to any messages

### Commenting Strategy:

**Good comments**:
- Add perspective: "I've seen this too, and what worked for us was..."
- Ask questions: "How did you handle the consent aspect of this?"
- Share experience: "This resonates - we had a similar situation where..."

**Bad comments**:
- Generic: "Great post!" "Agree!" "Thanks for sharing!"
- Self-promotional: "This is exactly what we do at [company]!"
- Argumentative: "Actually, you're wrong because..."

### Connection Request Strategy:

When sending requests, include a note:
- Reference something specific about them
- Explain why you want to connect
- Keep it brief (under 300 characters)

## Metrics to Track

### Weekly Metrics:

- Posts published: Target 3
- Impressions per post: Benchmark and improve
- Engagement rate: (Reactions + Comments) / Impressions
- New connections: Target 10-15
- DMs received: Quality conversations

### Monthly Metrics:

- Total follower growth
- Top-performing post (by engagement)
- Leads generated (DMs, meeting requests)
- Content themes that resonated

### Quarterly Metrics:

- Follower growth trend
- Engagement rate trend
- Lead quality and conversion

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Posting and ghosting** - Engagement in comments matters as much as the post
2. **Only promoting yourself** - 80% value, 20% promotion
3. **Ignoring DMs** - These are your warmest leads
4. **Inconsistency** - Algorithm rewards consistency
5. **Over-designing** - Simple, readable content beats fancy graphics
6. **External links in posts** - Algorithm penalises, put links in comments
7. **Engagement pods** - Fake engagement hurts more than helps
8. **Copying competitors** - Authenticity beats imitation

---

## Summary: The LinkedIn Formula

1. **Post consistently** (3x/week)
2. **Lead with value** (solve problems, share insights)
3. **Engage genuinely** (comment on others' content daily)
4. **Connect strategically** (grow your target audience)
5. **Convert softly** (occasional CTAs, mostly relationship-building)
6. **Track and adjust** (what works, do more; what doesn't, stop)
