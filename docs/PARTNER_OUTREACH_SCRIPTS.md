## Phone/Video Call Scripts

### Discovery Call Opening (5 minutes)

```
"Thanks for taking the time, [Name]. Before we dive in, I'd love to understand more about your work. 

Can you tell me a bit about the types of law firms you typically work with and the main challenges you help them solve?"

[Listen actively, take notes, ask follow-up questions]

"That's really helpful context. And when you're working with these firms, does documentation ever come up as a pain point? Things like attendance notes, meeting records, audit trails?"

[Listen for specific pain points]
```

### Your Pitch (3-5 minutes)

```
"So based on what you've shared, let me give you a quick overview of what we do and then we can explore whether there's a fit.

LegalNote helps solicitors turn client meetings into evidence-quality documentation. The core problem we solve is that attendance notes are often incomplete, written from memory, and impossible to verify later.

What we do differently:

1. Recording with built-in consent capture - so there's proof the client agreed
2. AI transcription with speaker identification - who said what, when
3. Automatic attendance note generation - structured, professional, immediate
4. Complete audit trail - cryptographically signed, tamper-proof

The firms using us typically cut documentation time by 50-70% and have a defensible record if anything's ever disputed.

What makes us different from just recording on your phone? The compliance layer. Everything is GDPR-compliant, SRA-ready, with the kind of audit trail that actually protects the solicitor.

Does that resonate with the types of challenges you see?"
```

### Partnership Proposal (5 minutes)

```
"So I wanted to explore whether there's a way we could work together that benefits your clients and creates value for you.

A few models I've seen work well:

Option 1: Referral Arrangement
You introduce us to firms where documentation is a gap. If they become customers, you receive 20-25% of the first year's subscription as commission. Completely hands-off after the intro.

Option 2: Co-Branded Workshop
We deliver a half-day workshop together - your methodology combined with our technology. You'd typically charge £1,000-1,500 for this, we split the fee. Your brand, your relationship, I provide the tech demo and documentation content.

Option 3: Bundled Offering
You include LegalNote as part of your consulting package. You buy licenses at a discounted rate and resell as part of your engagement. Increases your package value without extra delivery time.

What feels like it might fit best with how you work?"
```

### Handling Objections

**"I'm not sure my clients need this"**:
```
"That's fair. Can I ask - when you work with firms on their processes, do they ever mention challenges around:
- Keeping up with attendance notes?
- Proving what was discussed in a meeting?
- SRA compliance around record-keeping?

If those come up, LegalNote might be a natural recommendation. If not, I completely understand it's not the right fit."
```

**"I don't do referral arrangements"**:
```
"No problem at all. Would a collaboration on content be more interesting? Something like a joint article or webinar that positions both of us as experts? No commercial arrangement needed - just thought leadership that serves both our audiences."
```

**"I'd need to understand the product better"**:
```
"Absolutely. Would it help if I gave you a demo? I could also set you up with a trial account so you can see exactly how it works. Then you'd be in a position to know whether it's something worth mentioning to your clients."
```

## Partnership Agreement Key Terms (For Reference)

**Commission Model**:
- 20-25% of first year subscription revenue
- Paid quarterly in arrears
- 90-day attribution window from referral date
- Minimum 3-month customer retention required

**Co-Marketing Model**:
- Equal branding on materials
- Shared mailing list access (with consent)
- Cross-promotion on social channels
- Joint press release for significant milestones

**Reseller Model**:
- 30-40% discount on licenses
- Minimum annual commitment
- Partner provides first-line support
- Quarterly business reviews
