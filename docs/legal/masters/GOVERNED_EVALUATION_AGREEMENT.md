## 1. What this is

1.1	LegalNote grants the Firm access to the LegalNote platform for the Evaluation Period stated in the Key Terms, running from the date the Firm's account is configured, so that the Firm may evaluate it on real client matters. LegalNote shall confirm the configuration date to the Firm in writing, and the evaluation period runs from that date.

1.2	This is an evaluation, not a trial and not a pilot. The Firm uses the platform on live matters, under its own supervision, in the ordinary course of its practice. Nothing in this agreement is contingent on the Firm deciding to buy anything.

1.3	There is no charge. The Firm is under no obligation to purchase, and this agreement does not convert into a paid contract on expiry or at any other time.

1.4	Access is provided for up to the number of fee earners stated in the Key Terms, nominated by the Firm. The Firm may substitute a nominated fee earner at any time on notice to LegalNote.

## 2. Professional responsibility

This clause governs the whole of this agreement.

2.1	LegalNote is a documentation tool. It does not provide legal advice, does not provide legal services, and does not hold itself out as doing either. It is not a firm of solicitors and is not regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority. It carries on no reserved legal activity within the meaning of the Legal Services Act 2007.

2.2	Every document the platform produces is a draft. It is prepared from the record of the meeting and presented to the fee earner for review. It becomes the Firm's record only on Adoption. Until then it is not the Firm's record, and LegalNote does not represent it as such.

2.3	"Adoption" means the affirmative act by which a fee earner of the Firm confirms that they have read the draft, made any corrections they consider necessary, and adopted it as their own record. Adoption is recorded in the audit trail with the identity of the fee earner and the time at which it occurred. The platform will not release an unadopted document to a client or to any third party.

2.4	The fee earner remains responsible for the accuracy of the file, for the advice recorded in it, and for compliance with the SRA Standards and Regulations. The Firm remains responsible for supervision. LegalNote does not supervise, and does not replace supervision.

2.5	Where the platform marks a gap in the record, including where advice appears to have been given and no reason for it was stated, that marker is information for the fee earner. It is not a finding, and it is not a substitute for the fee earner's own judgement about what the file should say.

2.6	The Firm shall ensure that every fee earner using the platform understands clauses 2.1 to 2.5.

## 3. Data protection

3.1	The processing of personal data under this agreement is governed by the Data Processing Agreement between the parties. In the event of any conflict, that agreement prevails in respect of the processing of personal data, save as to liability, which is governed by clause 9 of this agreement.

3.2	The Firm's responsibilities in relation to consent, and the consent records the platform produces, are set out at clause 9 of the Data Processing Agreement.

3.3	The Data Processing Agreement must be accepted before the Firm's account is configured. No client data is processed before it is.

3.4	Before any client data is uploaded, the Firm confirms that it has satisfied itself that disclosure of client material to LegalNote is permitted under the SRA Code of Conduct and under its own terms of business, and that it has completed any data protection impact assessment it considers necessary.

## 4. What each party does

4.1	LegalNote shall: configure the Firm's account; provide a guided first use session; and provide support for the duration of the evaluation in accordance with clause 5.

4.2	The Firm shall: nominate fee earners up to the number stated in the Key Terms; nominate a contact for data protection matters; nominate a contact for operational matters; and give LegalNote its honest assessment of the platform.

4.3	If LegalNote identifies a risk to the security or integrity of the Firm's data, it may suspend the platform, or any part of it, immediately. It shall tell the Firm why, and shall restore the service as soon as it is safe to do so. Suspension under this clause is not a breach of this agreement.

## 5. If something goes wrong

5.1	A problem reported by the Firm goes directly to a named person and is answered by that person. It is not queued, triaged or routed, and the Firm will not be asked to raise a ticket. The contact is Jazz Dennis, jazz.dennis@legalnote.ai. LegalNote may nominate a different contact on written notice to the Firm.

5.2	Where a problem affects a client record, a live matter, or the Firm's ability to record a meeting, LegalNote shall acknowledge it within 4 working hours and shall respond substantively, with a fix or a workaround, the same working day. A problem reported outside working hours shall be acknowledged by 10am on the next working day.

5.3	Any other problem shall be acknowledged within 1 working day and answered within 2 working days.

5.4	Working hours are 9am to 6pm, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in England and Wales. LegalNote commits to acknowledgement and to response. It does not commit to resolution within a fixed period, because it will not promise a fix it cannot guarantee. Where a problem cannot be fixed the same day, LegalNote shall say so, shall provide a workaround if one exists, and shall tell the Firm when it expects to have a fix.

5.5	Where a recording fails to produce a record, the platform marks the matter as failed and shows it to the fee earner. LegalNote shall investigate without delay and confirm to the Firm, in writing, what happened and what if anything can be recovered.

5.6	On request, LegalNote shall give the Firm a written account of any incident affecting a client matter, together with the relevant entries from the audit trail, in a form the Firm may give to its supervisor, its COLP, its regulator or its insurer.

5.7	LegalNote shall notify the Firm in advance of any period during which the named contact will be unavailable, and shall nominate a substitute for that period.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1	Each party shall keep confidential the other's confidential information, and shall not disclose it without consent, save where required by law or by a regulator. This obligation continues without limit of time.

6.2	LegalNote shall not name the Firm as a client, a user, an evaluator or a reference without the Firm's prior written consent.

6.3	At the conclusion of the evaluation the Firm shall consider in good faith a request from LegalNote to be named as a design partner and to provide a reference. The Firm is under no obligation to agree.

## 7. Intellectual property and feedback

7.1	The platform, and all intellectual property in it, belongs to LegalNote and nothing in this agreement transfers any of it.

7.2	All documents, transcripts, notes and records produced from the Firm's matters belong to the Firm. To the extent that any intellectual property right in them vests in LegalNote, whether under section 9(3) of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 or otherwise, LegalNote assigns it to the Firm with full title guarantee, such assignment taking effect on creation. LegalNote shall not assert any such right.

7.3	LegalNote may use the Firm's feedback to improve the platform. It shall not use the content of any client matter to do so. LegalNote does not train, fine-tune or otherwise improve any machine learning model on the Firm's data, and shall procure that no sub-processor does so, as provided at clause 3.3 of the Data Processing Agreement.

7.4	The Firm grants LegalNote a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, without restriction or obligation, any feedback, suggestion or idea it provides. LegalNote owes no payment, attribution or obligation of confidence in respect of it. This clause permits no use of the content of any client matter.

7.5	The Firm warrants that it is entitled to provide to the platform the material it uploads, and to permit LegalNote to process it, and that in doing so it breaches no duty of confidence.

## 8. What LegalNote promises, and what it does not

8.1	LegalNote shall provide the platform with reasonable skill and care, and in accordance with the technical and organisational measures set out in the Data Processing Agreement.

8.2	The platform does not produce the Firm's record. It produces a draft, which becomes the Firm's record only on Adoption under clause 2. LegalNote gives no warranty that a draft is complete or correct, and the Firm does not rely on any such warranty. LegalNote's obligation in respect of a draft is the obligation at clause 8.1: to produce it with reasonable skill and care. Nothing in this clause excludes or limits LegalNote's liability for a failure to meet that obligation.

8.3	LegalNote does not warrant that the platform will be uninterrupted or error free.

8.4	LegalNote shall notify the Firm without undue delay of any defect which materially affects the integrity of a record produced from a client matter.

## 9. Liability

This clause governs liability under this agreement and under the Data Processing Agreement.

9.1	Subject to clause 9.4, LegalNote's aggregate liability under or in connection with this agreement and the Data Processing Agreement, taken together, is limited to £250,000.

9.2	The cap at clause 9.1 is a single aggregate cap. The caps are not cumulative, and the Firm may not recover more than once in respect of the same loss.

9.3	The cap at clause 9.1 applies to every claim by the Firm against LegalNote however arising, including any claim for contribution, recourse or indemnity, whether under Article 82(5) of the UK GDPR, the Civil Liability (Contribution) Act 1978, or otherwise.

9.4	Nothing in this agreement limits or excludes LegalNote's liability for:

(a)	death or personal injury caused by its negligence;

(b)	fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;

(c)	any breach of clause 4 of the Data Processing Agreement (legal professional privilege) arising from wilful misconduct; or

(d)	any liability which cannot lawfully be limited.

9.5	For the purposes of clause 9.4(c), "wilful misconduct" means an act or omission by a director of LegalNote done with actual knowledge that it constituted a breach of clause 4 of the Data Processing Agreement, or with reckless indifference as to whether it did so. It does not include any act or omission taken in good faith.

9.6	LegalNote maintains professional indemnity insurance and cyber and data insurance with Hiscox Insurance Company Limited, and shall maintain such cover, at a level no lower than that shown on the certificates provided to the Firm, for the term of this agreement. If the cover lapses or is materially reduced during the term, LegalNote shall tell the Firm immediately. Certificates of insurance are available to the Firm on request.

## 10. Ending it

10.1	Either party may end this agreement at any time, for any reason or none, on written notice. Neither owes the other anything on doing so. Termination does not affect any right or liability which has accrued before it.

10.2	The evaluation ends on the expiry of the Evaluation Period after the date confirmed under clause 1.1, unless the parties agree in writing to extend it or to enter into a paid agreement.

10.3	Before deletion, the Firm may export all of its data, including attendance notes, client letters, transcripts, consent records and the audit trail. LegalNote shall assist with the export at no charge.

10.4	If the Firm does not enter into a paid agreement, LegalNote shall delete all of the Firm's data and shall confirm the deletion in writing, in each case within 5 working days of the later of (a) the end of the evaluation and (b) the Firm's written confirmation that it has completed the export under clause 10.3. LegalNote shall give the Firm 5 working days' written notice before it deletes. This clause is subject to clauses 10.5 and 10.6 of the Data Processing Agreement.

10.5	Clauses 2, 6, 7, 9, 12 and this clause 10 survive the end of this agreement.

In entering into it, the Firm relies only on the statements set out in these two documents and on no other statement, whether written or oral. Nothing in this clause limits liability for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.

11.2	Each party acknowledges that this agreement is entered into for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which it acknowledges.

11.3	Neither party may assign this agreement without the other's written consent, save that LegalNote may assign it to a person acquiring the whole of its business. If LegalNote does so, or if there is a change in control of LegalNote, LegalNote shall notify the Firm, and the Firm may terminate this agreement and the Data Processing Agreement on written notice within 30 days, in which case clause 10.4 applies.

11.4	Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

11.5	This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

LegalNote retains the exact text corresponding to each recorded hash, so that the version accepted by the Firm can be reproduced. That record is the evidence of execution and of the version accepted.

12.3	By accepting, the representative confirms that they are authorised to enter into this agreement on the Firm's behalf, and that the details provided by the Firm are correct.

12.4	LegalNote Technologies Ltd, having made this agreement available for acceptance, is bound by it in respect of each Firm that accepts it.
