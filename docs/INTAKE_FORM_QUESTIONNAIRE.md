# LegalNote Pre-Call Intake Form

## Purpose
A short questionnaire sent to prospects before their discovery call. Helps you prepare and ensures the call is efficient and focused.

---

## Implementation Options

### Option A: Simple Email Reply
Send via email before the call — ask them to reply with answers.

### Option B: TypeForm / Google Form
Create an online form and include the link in your calendar invite.

### Option C: Calendly Pre-Call Questions
Add these as required questions when booking via Calendly.

## How to Use Responses

| Response | What it tells you | Call prep |
|----------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Mixed/General Practice** | Lower matter value likely | Entry-level pricing |
| **Commercial/M&A/Litigation** | Higher value matters | Premium positioning |
| **Previous documentation issues** | High compliance need | Emphasise audit trail, BlackBox |
| **"More than 1 hour" per meeting** | Strong time-saving case | Calculate ROI together |
| **Uses Clio** | Integration-ready | Show Clio integration |
| **"Critical" audit importance** | Compliance buyer | Lead with risk mitigation |
| **Immediate decision timeline** | Hot lead | Prioritise demo booking |
