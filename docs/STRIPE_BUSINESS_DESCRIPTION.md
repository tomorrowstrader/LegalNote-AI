## Company Overview

**LegalNote** is a UK-based SaaS platform providing legal documentation and meeting note automation tools for law firms and in-house legal teams. We serve solo practitioners, boutique law firms (1-10 solicitors), and corporate legal departments across the United Kingdom.

Our platform enables legal professionals to record client meetings, generate AI-powered transcripts, and produce compliant attendance notes and documentation - ensuring professional standards and regulatory compliance (SRA, GDPR).

## Products & Services

Our primary offering is a cloud-hosted legal documentation platform accessed via web browser. Customers subscribe on monthly or annual terms.

**Subscription Plans:**

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price | Target Customer |
|------|--------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Solo Plan | £99/month | £999/year | Solo practitioners, barristers, freelance solicitors |

- Unlimited audio recording and storage
