# LegalNote Discovery Call Script

## Purpose
A 15-20 minute conversation to understand the prospect's practice, documentation pain points, and pricing fit. The goal is to assess **value alignment** (not just firm size) to recommend appropriate pricing.

---

## Pre-Call Preparation (2 mins)

**Before the call, review:**
- Their website / LinkedIn profile
- Practice areas mentioned
- Firm size (if visible)
- Any notes from their early access signup

---

## Call Structure

### 1. Opening & Rapport (2-3 mins)

> "Thanks for taking the time to speak with me, [Name]. I'm [Your Name] from LegalNote. Before I dive into what we do, I'd love to learn a bit more about your practice so I can make sure we're a good fit."

**Transition question:**
> "Tell me about your firm — what type of work do you primarily handle?"

---

### 2. Practice Discovery (5-7 mins)

**Understanding their work (matter value indicators):**

| Question | What you're learning |
|----------|---------------------|
| "What's your typical matter worth in fees to the firm?" | Direct value indicator |
| "Are most of your matters fixed-fee or hourly?" | Billing model / margin sensitivity |
| "What's the average length of a matter?" | Complexity, documentation volume |
| "What proportion of your work involves client meetings?" | Usage frequency |

**Understanding their documentation workflow:**

| Question | What you're learning |
|----------|---------------------|
| "How do you currently document client meetings?" | Current pain point |
| "How much time does your team spend on attendance notes each week?" | Time cost |
| "Do you use dictation, typing, or both?" | Existing tools / habits |
| "Have you ever had a file note challenged in a complaint or claim?" | Compliance exposure |

---

### 3. Compliance & Risk Exposure (3-4 mins)

**Key questions to assess PI/compliance risk:**

> "Has your firm ever faced a situation where file documentation was questioned — either by a client, the SRA, or in negligence context?"

> "How do you currently document client consent before sensitive discussions?"

> "If a complaint came in tomorrow, how confident are you in your audit trail?"

**Listen for:**
- Past complaints or near-misses (high risk = higher value)
- SRA inspections or compliance concerns
- PI insurance renewal challenges
- Predecessor firm issues

---

### 4. Team & Workflow Fit (2-3 mins)

> "How many fee earners would be using LegalNote?"

> "Would you need everyone recording, or primarily client-facing team members?"

> "Do you currently use Clio, LEAP, or another case management system?"

> "Would you want calendar integration for meeting reminders?"

---

### 5. Pricing Conversation (3-4 mins)

**Based on what you've heard, position the price:**

**For high-value/high-risk practices (M&A, litigation, commercial):**
> "Given the nature of your work and the compliance exposure involved, most firms in your position invest between £500 and £1,500 per month. The value here is risk mitigation — having audit-ready records that protect against future claims."

**For mid-market boutiques (general practice, mixed work):**
> "For firms like yours handling a mix of work, we typically see investment in the £250-400 range monthly, which includes team collaboration and priority support."

**For solo practitioners / smaller matters:**
> "For solo practices, we've structured plans starting around £99-149 monthly — giving you everything you need without the overhead of team features."

---

### 6. Handling Objections

**"That's more than I expected"**
> "I understand. Let me ask — what's the cost of one successful negligence claim to your PI premium? For most firms, a single documented file note that prevents a complaint pays for years of LegalNote."

**"I need to think about it"**
> "Of course. Would it help if I sent you a summary of what we discussed, along with some information on how other [practice type] firms are using LegalNote?"

**"We're happy with our current process"**
> "That's great to hear. Out of curiosity — if a complaint came in next week, how quickly could you pull together a complete audit trail of a specific client matter?"

---

### 7. Next Steps (2 mins)

**If positive:**
> "Based on what you've told me, I think we're a strong fit. I'd suggest we set up a 30-minute demo where I can show you exactly how LegalNote would work for your practice. Would [day/time] work?"

**If uncertain:**
> "I'll send you a follow-up email with the key points we discussed and some case studies from similar firms. Feel free to reach out with any questions — and if you'd like to see a demo, just reply and we'll arrange a time."

---

## Pricing Decision Framework

| Signal | Suggested Anchor | Rationale |
|--------|------------------|-----------|
| Matters worth £20k+ | £800-1,500/month | Cost is trivial vs. matter value |
| Previous complaint/claim | £500-1,000/month | Proven compliance need |
| Team of 3+ fee earners | £300-500/month base + seats | Collaboration value |
| Mixed practice (varied matters) | £200-350/month | Balanced value |
| Solo, lower-value work | £99-149/month | Entry point |

---

## Post-Call Actions

1. **Send follow-up email within 24 hours** summarising:
   - Their pain points (in their words)
   - The pricing we discussed
   - Next steps / demo link

2. **Log in CRM:**
   - Practice type
   - Estimated matter value
   - Team size
   - Key pain points
   - Pricing discussed
   - Next action date

3. **Schedule demo** if agreed during call
